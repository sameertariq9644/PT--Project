The pathway

Six steps between you and a US licence — mapped clearly.

Most graduates don’t fail for lack of talent. They get lost in the order, the paperwork and the deadlines. This is the route, in plain language.

01Documents

Credential evaluation

Your DPT coursework is reviewed against US standards by an approved agency such as FCCPT. We prepare your file so gaps don’t surprise you.

02English

TOEFL iBT

Most states and credentialing bodies need TOEFL scores. We set your target by section and a timeline that fits your exam plan.

03Exam

The NPTE

The National Physical Therapy Exam by FSBPT. We build a study strategy, pick the right prep resources and time your attempt.

04Licence

State licensure

Every state board has its own rules and jurisprudence exam. Choosing the right state first can save you months.

05Immigration

VisaScreen & visa route

The Healthcare Worker Certificate, then the visa route that fits you — such as H-1B or an employment-based green card.

06Arrival

Job offer & relocation

Finding sponsoring employers, interviewing well, and landing ready to practise — from someone who has done it.

Requirements change and vary by state. All guidance is checked against current FSBPT, FCCPT and state board sources.
